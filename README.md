# Nopxnop 🎣

Lightweight, high-performance automation script for **Fish It!** (Roblox) featuring the clean and classic **Venyx UI** design.

---

## ⚡ Loadstring (Quick Run)

Paste and run the following line in your favorite executor (Delta, Fluxus, Hydrogen, Codex, Arceus X, Solara, Wave, MacSploit, etc.):

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Naufal-awafa/Nopxnop/main/Nopxnop.lua"))()
```

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎣 Auto Fishing
- **Auto Cast Rod**: Automatically equips and casts your fishing rod with customizable delay.
- **Cast Delay Slider**: Adjustable cooldown between casts (0.5s - 5.0s).
- **Auto Shake (Minigame)**: Automatically tracks and completes click/shake minigames instantly without lag.
- **Auto Reel / Fast Catch**: Instant reel simulation for rapid catches.
- **Auto Equip Rod**: Equips the best rod from your Backpack automatically.

### 💰 Economy & Auto Sell
- **Sell All Fish Now**: One-click remote sell.
- **Auto Sell Loop**: Automatically sells inventory periodically.
- **Adjustable Sell Interval**: Set auto-sell timer (10s to 180s).

### 🚀 Teleports
- **Islands & Key Locations**:
  - Starter Island / Spawn
  - Merchant / Sell Shop
  - Coral Reef Zone
  - Volcano / Magma Zone
  - Sunken Deep Trench
  - Mystic Ancient Island
  - Rod & Bait Vendor
- **Player Teleport**: Select any player from a dynamic dropdown and teleport directly to them.
- **AFK Safe Platform**: Spawns an invisible sky platform above the sea to safely fish AFK without drowning or mob aggro.

### 🏃 Player & Movement
- **WalkSpeed Slider**: Customize player movement speed (16 to 150).
- **JumpPower Slider**: Customize jump height (50 to 250).
- **Infinite Jump**: Jump continuously in mid-air.
- **Noclip**: Walk freely through walls and obstacles.
- **Infinite Oxygen / Anti-Drown**: Stay underwater indefinitely.
- **Anti-AFK**: Bypasses the 20-minute Roblox idle kick.

### 👁️ Visuals & Settings
- **FullBright / No Fog**: Enhances visibility in deep waters and night time.
- **Keybind**: Press `RightShift` to toggle the Venyx menu.
- **Rejoin Server**: Instant quick reconnect.
- **Unload**: Safely disconnects all loops and cleanly removes the UI.
